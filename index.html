Metropolitan Naga Public Employment Office

The Metropolitan Public Employment Service Office (Metro PESO) of Naga City serves as the primary gateway for Nagueños to access meaningful employment opportunities, career guidance, and workforce development initiatives. Operating under its Manpower Development and Placement Division, Metro PESO is dedicated to empowering the local community by bridging the gap between job seekers, students, and public or private opportunities across the city.

Empowering the Youth: The Youth Employment Program

To directly support the younger generation of Nagueños, Metro PESO spearheads the Youth Employment Program (YEP). YEP serves as the overarching umbrella initiative designed to provide temporary employment, educational assistance, and hands-on professional exposure to deserving youth across Naga City. Through strategic partnerships with City Hall departments, barangay units, and partner offices, YEP equips young workers with essential workplace skills while helping ease the financial burden of their education.

WHAT YOU CAN AVAIL WITH YEP

PROGRAMS OFFERED

Two programs designed to provide temporary employment and financial assistance to deserving youth across Naga City.

SEFE

Summer Employment for Enrollment

SEFE provides temporary employment to qualified students during school breaks, helping them earn income to prepare for the upcoming academic year.

  • For high school and college students
  • Short-term summer employment opportunities
  • Hands-on experience in government offices
  • Stipend based on current city guidelines

SPES

Special Program for Employment of Students

SPES provides temporary employment to qualified students, helping them gain work experience, develop skills, and support their education.

  • For high school and college students
  • Short-term employment opportunities
  • Hands-on experience in government offices
  • Stipend based on current guidelines
Note: Both programs require physical submission of documentary requirements at the Metro PESO Office, City Hall, Naga City. Online document submission is not accepted.

Who Can Apply?

Eligibility criteria for SPES & SEFE as of 2026
  • Enrolled or incoming Grade 12, 1st-year college, or higher college students
  • Out-of-School Youth (OSY)
  • Dependents of low-income or displaced workers
  • Family income does not exceed ₱146,766 per year
  • GWA of 85% or higher with no failing grades
  • Financial aid does not exceed ₱5,000 per semester
  • Aged 18–30 (priority, especially solo parent or PWD dependents)

Disqualification Grounds

Applicants disqualified if they:
  • Drop any subject during the program period
  • Receive non-RA 10931 financial aid exceeding ₱5,000/semester
  • Are graduating college students
  • Are enrolled in summer classes or OJT
  • Fail to submit complete requirements before the deadline
  • Have another family member currently enrolled in the program

Documentary Requirements Checklist

  • Duly accomplished SPES / SEFE Application Form
  • PSA Birth Certificate / Local Civil Registry Copy
  • Form 138 (Report Card) or Official Transcript of Records (GWA of at least 85%)
  • Barangay Certificate of Indigency
  • Parents' Income Tax Return (ITR) or Tax Exemption Certificate
  • Certificate of Non-Receipt of Financial Aid exceeding ₱5,000
